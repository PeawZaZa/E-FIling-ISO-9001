## 2. Report structure

The report has five parts in this order: front matter (`ส่วนนำ`), body (`ส่วนเนื้อความ`), references and/or bibliography (`ส่วนอ้างอิงและบรรณานุกรม`), appendices (`ส่วนภาคผนวก`), and author biography (`ประวัติผู้เขียน`). Cited references and related but uncited bibliography are distinct; if both are present, make separate lists at the end of the body. [§§2, 2.1–2.5, pp. 34–50; §2.3, pp. 37–38]

## 3. Front matter

- Order: outer cover, inner cover, examination committee approval page, Thai abstract, English abstract, acknowledgements, contents, list of figures, list of tables, and explanation of symbols/abbreviations (when used). The manual also mentions definitions/technical terms; its specimen has separate symbol and terminology/abbreviation lists. [§§2.1, 3.4, pp. 34–35, 52; specimens pp. 71, 75–76]
- Outer cover: white hard cover; full-colour university emblem; black text and numbers. Use TH Sarabun New, 20 pt for the project title and 18 pt for other cover text. The spine uses black TH Sarabun New 16 pt and shows the project title and academic year. Follow the manual's cover/spine layouts: no name prefix for authors; Thai academic year without `พ.ศ.`. The English inner-cover specimen uses a Gregorian academic year without `ค.ศ.`. [§§2.1.1–2.1.2, p. 34; specimens pp. 60–63]
- Approval page: Thai; include signatures of the advisor and examination committee, and spell out academic ranks. The specimen also shows the programme head's signature and form fields. [§2.1.3, p. 34; specimens pp. 66–67]
- Thai abstract: concise purpose/scope, key principles or theory, method (including tools and study group where applicable), and results (including statistical significance if applicable). English abstract conveys the same meaning; the manual recommends past tense for purpose/method and present tense for results. The Thai abstract specimen specifies 3–5 comma-separated keywords. [§§2.1.4–2.1.5, pp. 34–35; specimens pp. 68–69]
- Acknowledgements follow the English abstract and are no longer than one page. Contents and figure/table lists show page locations; if continued, center `สารบัญ (ต่อ)`, `สารบัญรูป (ต่อ)`, or `สารบัญตาราง (ต่อ)` on the next page. Symbols/abbreviations, if used, must be consistent throughout and arranged alphabetically. [§§2.1.6–2.1.10, p. 35]

## 4. Main content and chapter structure

The manual describes **five common content parts**, while allowing the body to be divided into several chapters; its specimen contents displays Chapters 1–5. Exact project-specific chapter titles and subsections are **Not specified / requires confirmation** rather than fixed by the specimen. [§2.2, pp. 35–37; specimen pp. 71–72]

1. Introduction: problem/background, feasible objectives, hypothesis if applicable, scope, and expected benefits. [§2.2.1, pp. 35–36]
2. Related theory and work: relevant principles and prior research/projects; cite borrowed information, tables, and figures. Quantity and quality of related work are subject to advisor/programme judgement. [§2.2.2, p. 36]
3. Method/design/experiment/research procedure: explain design, data collection, tests or other work sufficiently for the reader to understand the process and material error or safety concerns. [§2.2.3, p. 36]
4. Results and discussion: report results against the stated methods, organise the discussion clearly, and use graphs, tables, and figures where they help. Discuss results against relevant theory and limitations. [§2.2.4, pp. 36–37]
5. Conclusions and recommendations: conclude against each objective in order, state limitations and causes where applicable, and ground recommendations in actual project results. [§2.2.5, p. 37]

## 5. Typography and language

- Body text: black TH Sarabun New 16 pt, with Thai distributed justification (`กระจายแบบไทย`) consistently throughout. Equation text/variables: italic Times New Roman 12 pt; equation symbols: Symbol 14 pt. [§§3.1, 3.8, pp. 51, 56]
- Use the current Royal Institute dictionary for Thai spelling and its technical terminology for academic terms. Preserve original spellings for foreign personal/proper names such as software and devices; use one accepted spelling consistently if several are valid. [§3.1, p. 51]
- Caption, footnote, quotation, and bibliography font sizes beyond the general body rule: **Not specified / requires confirmation**. [§3.1, p. 51; §§3.6–3.7, pp. 53–56]

## 6. Paper, page layout, and spacing

- Print single-sided on plain, white, smooth, uncoated A4 paper of 80 gsm. Margins: top 1.5 in, left 1.5 in, right 1 in, bottom 1 in. Avoid splitting a word at a line break. [§§3.2–3.3, p. 51; layout specimens pp. 64–65]
- Chapter name is one line below `บทที่ N`; leave one line before a major heading and one blank line above and below an equation. The approval and abstract specimens show additional local blank-line placements. [§§3.5.1–3.5.2, p. 52; §3.8, pp. 56–57; specimens pp. 66, 68–69]
- General line spacing, ordinary paragraph first-line indent, paragraph before/after spacing, and text-alignment exceptions: **Not specified / requires confirmation**. The specimen's visual spacing is not an explicit numerical rule. [§§3.1, 3.3–3.5, pp. 51–53; specimens pp. 64–65]

## 7. Page numbering

- Front matter uses Thai consonant letters in sequence, beginning with the Thai abstract; no visible letter on the inner cover, Thai approval page, or first contents page. The manual does not state an exact first letter, and the contents specimen shows the Thai abstract as `ง`. **Exact starting letter and whether omitted pages consume letters: Not specified / requires confirmation.** [§3.4, p. 52; specimen p. 71]
- Body, reference/bibliography, appendices, and biography use one continuous Arabic sequence (`1, 2, 3, …`) through the last page. Place visible numbers 1 in from the top edge, aligned with the right text margin, in normal 16 pt type. Omit the visible number, but still count the page, on the first page of each body chapter, the references/bibliography, each appendix, and the biography. [§3.4, p. 52]

## 8. Chapters and headings

- Each chapter starts on a new page. Center `บทที่ N` near the top and the chapter name one line below; use bold 18 pt and no underline. A long chapter name may occupy 2–3 lines. [§3.5.1, p. 52]
- Major headings: left aligned, bold 16 pt, numbered `N.1`, `N.2`, etc.; leave one line before the heading. Move a heading to the next page if no more than one line of following text would fit. [§§3.5.2–3.5.3, pp. 52–53]
- Subheadings: bold 16 pt. First sublevel (`N.1.1`) starts 0.5 in (1.27 cm) from the left text margin; deeper levels use `N.1.1.1`, then `1)`, `2)`, etc., aligned as illustrated. [§3.5.4, p. 53; specimen p. 64]

## 9. Tables

- Normally keep table number, title, content, and source on one page; landscape orientation is permitted. Table contents use TH Sarabun New 12 pt. [§3.6.1, p. 53]
- Put `ตารางที่` plus chapter-based number and title **above** the table, aligned with the left text margin. Number consecutively within each chapter (`ตารางที่ 1.1`, `2.1`); appendices use the appendix letter (`ตารางที่ ก.1`). Bold `ตารางที่` and the number. [§3.6.2, p. 54]
- Cite an externally sourced table below it. Keep tables within the page frame and legible; oversized tables that cannot be reduced are for the advisor to decide. If a table continues, repeat its number with `(ต่อ)` on the next page (`ตารางที่ 1.1 (ต่อ)`). [§§3.6.3–3.6.5, pp. 54–55]

## 10. Figures

- Figures include images, maps, diagrams, charts, and graphs. Keep them clear and sized for the page; text/numbers inside use TH Sarabun New 16 pt. [§3.7.1, p. 55]
- Place number and title/description **below** each figure, centered. Number within the chapter (`รูปที่ 1.1`, `2.1`), or by appendix (`รูปที่ ก.1`); bold `รูปที่` and the number. Cite an externally sourced figure below it, as the manual's example shows. [§§3.7.2, 3.7.4–3.7.5, pp. 55–56]
- Reproduce figures clearly on paper rather than attaching them with glue. The manual applies table printing practice to figures. [§§3.7.3–3.7.4, p. 55]

## 11. Equations

- Center equations; use italic Times New Roman 12 pt for equation text/variables and Symbol 14 pt for mathematical symbols. Leave one blank line before and after. [§§3.1, 3.8, pp. 51, 56]
- Number each equation consecutively within its chapter using normal type in parentheses, e.g. `(1.1)`, `(2.1)`; use appendix lettering for appendix equations, e.g. `(ก.1)`. Define variables and units as shown in the manual example. [§3.8, pp. 56–57]

## 12. Citations and references

- **Advisor decision required:** the manual permits CMS/Chicago or APA for the whole report; the advisor chooses one, and it must remain consistent. Do not select a system from an example report. Cited sources and related but uncited bibliography are different; if both are included, list them separately. [§2.3, pp. 37–38]
- **CMS/Chicago as described by this manual:** number references in first-citation order and cite those numbers in square brackets in text (e.g. `[1]`); a separate uncited bibliography is alphabetical. The manual supplies formats for journals, books, proceedings, theses, dictionaries, newspapers, electronic sources, and standards. For electronic sources, record source/site and retrieval date. [§§2.3.1–2.3.1.9, pp. 38–43]
- **APA as described by this manual:** in-text author–year citation (e.g. `(Garvin, 1988)`); alphabetic reference list, with later lines of an entry indented about 5–7 characters or 0.5 in. The manual explicitly directs unresolved APA details to **APA 6th edition**. Its §§2.3.2.5–2.3.2.6 give formats/examples by source type, including electronic sources with retrieval information and URL. Personal communications are cited only in text, not the reference list. [§§2.3.2–2.3.2.6, pp. 44–50]
- Bibliographic examples in the manual illustrate formatting; they are not sources for E-Filing claims. [§2.3, pp. 37–38]

## 13. Appendices

Start with a new centered `ภาคผนวก` divider page. For multiple appendices, start the next page with centered `ภาคผนวก ก` and the appendix name on the following line; continue with Thai letters (`ก`, `ข`, `ค`, …). Place supporting material too detailed for the body there, such as calculations, larger data sets, symbol tables, and charts. Appendix tables, figures, and equations use the appendix letter in their numbers. [§2.4, p. 50; §§3.6.2, 3.7.2, 3.8, pp. 54–57]

## 14. Author biography

Place biographies last, no more than one page per author; if there is more than one author, give each a separate page. Include name (bold, with appropriate prefix/title), student ID, date of birth, education from upper secondary/vocational level through bachelor's, institutions and graduation years, significant experience/academic work/awards/scholarships, and contact details. Include a photograph; the manual recommends graduation attire, and its specimen shows 3 × 4 cm. [§2.5, p. 50; §3.9, p. 57; specimen p. 77]

## 15. Submission and final report

- Before the project examination, submit the draft thesis through the advisor and course coordinator; the coordinator's review includes a Turnitin plagiarism check. A similarity result over 30% requires revision and another review under this manual's stated process. After the draft is accepted for examination, prepare copies for the examiners and course coordinator. [§§1.10.3–1.10.5, p. 18]
- After passing, submit at least one signed final thesis volume (examination committee and programme head) and one electronic data set to the course coordinator. If corrections are required, the advisor must check them within 14 days of the announced examination result before final submission. [§1.8.2, p. 7; §1.10.9.1, p. 19]
- Section 1.8.2 says one final printed volume, whereas §1.10.9.1 says at least one. The process flowchart calls the electronic set a CD, whereas those sections say only electronic media/data. **Exact final copy count beyond the minimum, current medium, file format, filename, and any required upload: Not specified / requires confirmation.** [§1.8.2, p. 7; §1.10.9.1 and flowchart, pp. 19–21]

## 16. Rules for Codex when drafting

- Read this file first. Open the cited manual page only if a rule is ambiguous, a page template must be reproduced, or a later manual revision appears. Never infer official rules from `docs/report-examples/`.
- Use `PROJECT_STATE.md` and relevant E-Filing sources for facts; distinguish requirements, design, implementation, test evidence/results, limitations, and unresolved decisions. Do not invent facts, statistics, citations, or results.
